# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Tabby to be a welcoming, respectful, and productive open-source project. Everyone participating in the project—online or in person—is expected to help create an environment where people can contribute regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code, not personal characteristics.
- Give and receive feedback with empathy and specific examples.
- Respect different viewpoints, experiences, and communication styles.
- Protect private information, including browsing data and security reports.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize when appropriate, and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith arguments.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, private workspace content, or vulnerability details in public channels.
- Any conduct that a reasonable person would consider unsafe or inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, community spaces, and public interactions where someone represents Tabby.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's private reporting channel. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal or security information. If the available channel would involve the subject of the report, contact another maintainer directly.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, protect confidentiality where reasonably possible, and respond in proportion to the behavior. Responses may include a private warning, content removal, temporary participation restrictions, or a permanent ban. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is not permitted.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct may be removed from project responsibilities through the project's governance process.
